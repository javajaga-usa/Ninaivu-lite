Consolidate drives into one archive

Sweeps photos and video off however many old drives, cards and backup folders you have into one archive filed as YYYY/MM/DD. Sources are opened read-only and nothing is ever deleted — every copy is re-read from disk and SHA-256 matched before it counts.
